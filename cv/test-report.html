B · Unseen — does it generalise?

White Toyota Aygo, plate WI 022LC, front-left impact. Checklist R-01…R-07. No second human inventory; we compare tags to what the photos actually are.

Interpretation

  • Subject tagging mostly transfers. Front / left / rear / bumper close-ups land on the right ids without a Qashqai-specific enum.
  • R-04 never appears on any file. The “right side spared” shot (1174) was called left. Same class of error as gold R-05/R-06: viewpoint/side confusion.
  • Findings generalise well. Damage, plate, Toyota/Aygo, leak, Mercedes frame on a Toyota — documentary detail the checklist did not name, which is what we asked for.
  • constraint_ok is still one bit per photo. 1171 sets it false talking about R-07 “from above” while also claiming R-05 (plate). A photo that serves two constrained ids cannot store two answers in one flag.

So: the module does generalise as a “describe this photo against a checklist” engine. It does not yet generalise the hard spatial judgements (between seats, inside vs outside, left vs right, true overhead). Those are exactly the constraints that decide pass/fail when the folder already “looks complete.”
